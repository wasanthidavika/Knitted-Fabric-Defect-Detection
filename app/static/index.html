What do the numbers mean?

Score is the detector's confidence ranking. It is not a probability and not a guarantee. Seen in validation shows how often detections with a similar score were correct on held-out validation images (with the number of examples). On new fabric or a different camera it can be lower.

Why was this flagged?

Highlighted areas show where the model is sensitive. They do not prove why a defect exists or how the model reasons.
